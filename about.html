About Me

I'm a product designer and artist working independently from Redding, CA. I'm a design generalist with varied skills that fall into four main categories.

Background

I'm passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences that combine beautiful aesthetics with intuitive functionality. With expertise spanning product design, graphic design, typography, and 3D artwork, I bring a holistic approach to every project.

Whether it's crafting user interfaces for mobile apps, developing brand identities for startups, creating custom typefaces, or producing stunning 3D visuals, I strive to deliver work that exceeds expectations.

Skills & Expertise

My design practice is built on a foundation of versatility. I believe that the best solutions come from understanding multiple disciplines and knowing how to apply them effectively.

Figma Framer Photoshop Illustrator InDesign Glyphs Cinema 4D Redshift

Approach

I approach each project with curiosity and attention to detail. From initial concept to final delivery, I focus on creating work that not only looks great but also solves real problems and creates value.

Collaboration is key to my process. I work closely with clients to understand their vision, goals, and constraints, ensuring that the final result aligns perfectly with their needs.

What I Do

Product Design

Using Figma and Framer to explore and create user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity screens for web and device apps.

Graphic Design

Using Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign to design everything from albums, singles and merch to full corporate brands.

Typography

Using Illustrator and Glyphs app to create custom wordmarks, titles, and typography as well as full retail typefaces.

3D Artwork

Creating a variety of personal and commercial artwork using Cinema 4D with Redshift.
